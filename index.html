04 / BITCOIN SECURITY

Protect your Bitcoin

Self-custody safety

Practical checks for protecting a Bitcoin wallet, recovery phrase and transactions. This section is educational: it does not connect to wallets, sign transactions or hold funds.

Recovery phrase

  • Never type your 12/24-word recovery phrase into a website, chat, form or support message.
  • Never photograph it or store it in cloud notes, email or screenshots.
  • Keep offline backups in secure, separate places. Protect them from theft, fire and water.
  • Anyone who gets the phrase can usually take the funds. Real support staff never need it.

Wallet & device

  • Download wallet software only from the project's official site; verify signatures or checksums when provided.
  • Keep your operating system, wallet and hardware-wallet firmware updated.
  • Use a device PIN and a strong unique password where supported.
  • Consider a hardware wallet for significant savings; practise recovery before depositing funds.

Before sending

  • Verify the full receiving address on the wallet device screen, not only the computer screen.
  • Clipboard malware can replace copied addresses. Check the first and last characters and independently verify the entire address.
  • Send a small test transaction first when appropriate; confirm network fees and destination.
  • Bitcoin transactions are generally irreversible. Confirm details before signing.

Scam warning signs

  • Anyone promising guaranteed returns, urgent giveaways or doubling your Bitcoin is a scam risk.
  • Ignore unsolicited support messages and sponsored search results impersonating wallet providers.
  • Never share a recovery phrase, private key, wallet backup or remote access to your device.
  • Do not install unknown wallet apps, browser extensions or remote-support tools.
